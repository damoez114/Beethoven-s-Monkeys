Beethoven · Symphony No. 5 in C minor, Op. 67

Monkey Symphony

A full orchestra of 58 monkeys, the size of a typical performance, each hitting random notes in their instrument's range at random moments. Given enough time they will play the whole Fifth. First they need the opening four notes: G, G, G, E♭.

Every wait between notes is random, a second or two on average, and each monkey picks any note in its instrument's range. Pitches are sounding pitch.

The timpani are tuned to G and C, as in Beethoven's score, so those two drums can only ever play those two notes. The piccolo, contrabassoon and trombones only join in the finale. There is no contrabassoon in the sample set, so that monkey plays bassoon samples an octave lower.
