Beethoven · Symphony No. 5 in C minor, Op. 67

Monkey Symphony

Fifteen monkeys, one for each part of the score, each hitting random notes in their instrument's range at random moments. Given enough time they will play the whole Fifth. First they need the opening four notes: G, G, G, E♭.

Sound plays at 1× speed only. Fifteen monkeys at once is noisy.

Every wait between notes is random, a second or two on average, and each monkey picks any note in its instrument's range. Pitches are sounding pitch.

The timpani are tuned to G and C, as in Beethoven's score, so those two drums can only ever play those two notes. The piccolo, contrabassoon and trombones only join in the finale.
